# 🌱 Greenhouse Plant Growth

Welcome to **Greenhouse Plant Growth**! This dataset captures plant growth patterns in a controlled greenhouse environment, providing insights into environmental factors, growth rates, and optimization techniques for various plant species.

## 📌 Dataset Overview
- ☀ **Environmental Conditions:** Tracks temperature, humidity, and sunlight exposure  
- 🌿 **Plant Growth Metrics:** Height, leaf count, biomass, and root development  
- 💦 **Watering & Nutrient Levels:** Irrigation schedules and soil nutrient concentrations  
- 📈 **Growth Rate Analysis:** Comparisons of different species under greenhouse conditions  

## 📂 Data Format
The dataset is structured in CSV format with key attributes:
- `Plant ID` – Unique identifier for each plant sample  
- `Species` – Name of the plant species  
- `Growth Stage` – Seedling, vegetative, flowering, etc.  
- `Height (cm)` – Recorded plant height over time  
- `Leaf Count` – Number of leaves present  
- `Temperature (°C)` – Greenhouse temperature during measurement  
- `Humidity (%)` – Atmospheric humidity level
